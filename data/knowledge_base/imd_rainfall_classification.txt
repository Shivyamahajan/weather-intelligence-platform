Question: What rainfall amount is considered heavy rainfall
according to IMD?

Answer:
According to the IMD classification used in this project,
heavy rainfall is 64.5–115.5 mm in 24 hours.

Question: What rainfall amount is considered very heavy rainfall?

Answer:
Very heavy rainfall is 115.6–204.4 mm in 24 hours.

Question: What rainfall amount is considered extremely heavy
rainfall?

Answer:
Extremely heavy rainfall is 204.5 mm or more in 24 hours.

Question: What are the IMD rainfall categories?

Answer:
The rainfall intensity categories used in the project are:
- Light rainfall
- Moderate rainfall
- Heavy rainfall
- Very heavy rainfall
- Extremely heavy rainfall

INDIA METEOROLOGICAL DEPARTMENT — RAINFALL CLASSIFICATION

Daily Rainfall Classification (mm per day):
- No Rain: 0 - 2.4 mm
- Very Light Rain: 2.5 - 7.4 mm
- Light Rain: 7.5 - 35.4 mm
- Moderate Rain: 35.5 - 64.4 mm
- Rather Heavy Rain: 64.5 - 115.5 mm
- Heavy Rain: 115.6 - 204.4 mm
- Very Heavy Rain: 204.5 - 244.4 mm
- Extremely Heavy Rain: More than 244.5 mm

Weekly Rainfall Categories:
- Excess: more than 60% above normal
- Large Excess: more than 60% below normal
- Normal: -19% to +19% of normal
- Deficient: -20% to -59% below normal
- Large Deficient: more than 60% below normal

Southwest Monsoon Season: June to September
Northeast Monsoon Season (Tamil Nadu/Andhra): October to December
Pre-Monsoon Season: March to May
Winter Season: January to February

Key Facts:
- India receives about 75-90% of its annual rainfall during 
  the Southwest Monsoon season (June-September)
- Average annual rainfall across India: approximately 1200mm
- Western Ghats and Northeast India receive the highest rainfall
- Rajasthan and parts of Gujarat receive the lowest rainfall

Warnings Issued by IMD:
- Yellow Warning: Heavy Rainfall expected (64.5 - 115.5 mm)
- Orange Warning: Very Heavy Rainfall expected (115.6 - 204.4 mm)
- Red Warning: Extremely Heavy Rainfall expected (>204.4 mm)
